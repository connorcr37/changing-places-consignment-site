Start With Priorities

Furnish the Way You Actually Live

A new address can make every empty corner feel urgent. Start with the rooms you use most and let the rest come together naturally.

First-time homeowners and renters

Begin with the pieces that support daily life: a comfortable place to sit, eat, work, sleep, and store essentials. Leave secondary rooms open until you understand how you use the space.

Relocations and busy professionals

Bring a short room list and measurements so you can quickly focus on pieces that have a place.

Combined or refreshed households

Decide what both households are keeping before adding more. A distinctive table, rug, mirror, or chair can bridge existing pieces without replacing everything that already works.

Measure the Whole Route

Make Sure the Piece Can Reach the Room

Measure the room, the tightest doorway or turn, and your vehicle. Save the numbers and a room photo on your phone — we'll help you measure and compare pieces in the showroom.

  • The room and the available wall space
  • The tightest doorway, hall, stair, elevator, or turn
  • Your vehicle's cargo opening and usable space
  • A room photo or simple floor plan saved on your phone

Bring a tape measure

Measure the item in the store and compare it with the smallest point along the route. We'll be glad to help.

If you'd like help with transport, ask us for a trusted full-service mover recommendation.

Start With What Matters Most

Make Every Shopping Trip Count

You don't need to furnish the whole home at once. Start with the essentials, then add pieces as you find them.

Start with the big pieces

Look first for the sofa, dining table, bed frame, or storage piece that gives the room its main function.

Live with the layout

Live with the basics for a bit. You'll quickly see where people walk and what the room still needs.

Add personality

Return for lamps, rugs, mirrors, wall art, occasional tables, and decor that connect the rooms without making them feel like a matching set.

Keep a flexible list

Keep measurements, colors, and maximum sizes on your phone. You'll know right away what can work.
