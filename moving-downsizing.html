Plan Before You Pack

An Easier Way to Sort the Furniture

Start with your move date and a few simple groups. Not every decision has to happen at once.

Measure the next space

Start with floor plans, room dimensions, doorways, and the furniture that must stay. This keeps sentimental or high-quality pieces from moving twice when they won't fit.

Make a few simple groups

Sort into keep, offer to family, consider for consignment, sell another way, and donate. A meaningful piece can still be ready for a new home.

Text us before you move anything

For a few items, send clear photos while they're easy to see. If you have a whole house or a large group, describe the scope first — we may be able to arrange a quick look instead of asking for hundreds of photos.

Make one transport plan

We'll tell you what may work and arrange a drop-off time. Bring approved pieces in the right vehicle, or ask us for a trusted mover recommendation.

Choose a quick route for the rest

Use a direct sale, donation, estate sale, or clean-out option for anything that needs to leave by a firm date.

What to Send

What Helps in a Text

A few clear details help us give you a useful answer before you spend time moving anything.

  • One full view showing the entire item
  • Side, back, and detail views when shape or construction matters
  • Manufacturer labels or maker marks, if available
  • Close-ups of wear, stains, chips, scratches, or repairs
  • Basic dimensions and quantity for large sets or multiple pieces
  • Your general timeline, especially if a move date is approaching

Clean and accessible beats packed away

Items should arrive clean, in good condition, and ready for the sales floor — free of dirt, dust, stains, odors, and pet hair. Photographing and cleaning before the moving truck arrives is usually easier than reopening boxes or a packed storage unit.

Two Kinds of Timeline

Match the Selling Method to the Deadline

We display approved pieces for up to four months, so match each item to the time you have.

If you have several weeks or more

Text us early, clean the approved pieces, and arrange transport. You'll still have time to choose another route for anything we can't use.

A mix often works best: consign some pieces, sell or donate others, and keep the overall move on schedule.

If the property must be clear soon

Share your date in the first text. Consignment may still work for pieces that can come in soon. For anything that has to go right away, choose a faster option.

A mover, estate-sale company, donation group, or clean-out service can help with the rest.

Need help moving approved pieces?

Bring them yourself or ask us to recommend a trusted full-service mover. We don't offer pickup, so wait for our reply before paying to move anything.

Keep the Transition Moving

A Simple Decision Filter for Each Piece

Will it fit?

Check physical dimensions, room function, and whether the piece supports the way the next home will be used.

Is it wanted?

Ask family members with a clear response deadline. Open-ended offers can stall the rest of the move.

Is it sale ready?

Condition, style, category, and current demand all help us decide whether consignment may work.

What's the next best route?

Choose the next route before the move date: direct sale, estate sale, auction, donation, recycling, or disposal.
