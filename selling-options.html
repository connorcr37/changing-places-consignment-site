Choosing How to Sell

Consignment vs. Facebook Marketplace and Other Ways to Sell

There's no single right way to sell furniture. The best choice is the one that fits your time, timeline, and comfort level. Here's a quick, honest comparison.

Compare the options How we work

The Core Difference

Sell It Yourself — or Let Us Handle the Sale?

Selling it yourself gives you more control. Consigning with us takes messages, negotiations, and buyer appointments off your plate.

If you sell it yourself

  • Clean, stage, measure, and photograph the item.
  • Write and refresh the listing.
  • Answer messages, screen buyers, and negotiate.
  • Manage no-shows, rescheduling, payment, and pickup.
  • Decide whether and when strangers may enter your home.

If you consign with us

  • Text us a few photos before making a trip.
  • Bring approved pieces to the store.
  • We price, style, and display them in our busy showroom.
  • We handle shopper questions and the transaction.
  • Receive 45% of the final sale price if the item sells.

The Hidden Work

What a DIY Furniture Listing Actually Requires

Posting can take minutes. Managing the sale often takes much longer.

Photography and listing upkeep

Useful listings need clear angles, dimensions, condition details, an accurate description, and a price. If interest slows, you may revise, repost, or add more information.

Messages, no-shows, and negotiation

You may answer the same questions again and again, figure out who's serious, respond to offers, and start over after a no-show.

Safety and pickup

You decide what contact information to share, where to meet, who may enter the home, how payment is handled, and whether the buyer has enough people and the right vehicle.

A Fair Choice

When Another Option Makes More Sense

Consignment can be one easy part of the plan, especially when a home has a mix of furniture, everyday goods, and specialty pieces.

Choose Marketplace or Craigslist when…

You want to set your own price, keep the direct-sale proceeds, respond quickly, and manage pickup yourself. It can work well for a practical item priced to move.

Choose a tag or moving sale when…

You have many smaller or lower-priced household items, can host shoppers, and want a single event before donating or disposing of leftovers.

Choose an estate sale when…

A large share of a household needs to be inventoried, staged, sold, and cleared together. Read the provider's scope, fees, minimums, and post-sale plan carefully.

Choose consignment when…

You have approved quality furniture, time for a showroom sale, and would rather let our team price, style, and sell it than manage shoppers yourself.

A mix is completely normal

You might consign selected furniture, take collectibles to a specialist, and donate everyday items. Our moving and downsizing and estate furniture guides can help.
