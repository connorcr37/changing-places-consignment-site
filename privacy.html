Website Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026.

Overview

Changing Places Consignment Shop ("Changing Places," "we," "our," or "us") uses this website to share store information, consignment details, recent highlights, location information, contact options, and photo submissions for consignment review. It does not offer checkout or payment collection.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or interact with this website. This website is intended for general audiences. You may choose to provide contact information and photos for a consignment review.

Information You Choose To Send

This website includes contact options such as phone, text, and email links. When you use those links, your device may open your phone, messaging, or email app.

Any information you choose to send through phone, text, email, or another communication channel is sent by you to Changing Places through that channel. This may include your name, contact information, message contents, photos, or any other information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to you, review potential consignments, provide requested services, manage an existing business relationship, and maintain appropriate business records.

Web Photo Submissions

When you submit photos for review, we collect your name, phone number and/or email address, optional notes, and selected photos. Photos are resized before upload and are stored privately with your submission. Our staff use these details to evaluate possible consignments and follow up with you. The store receives a private email with your contact details, notes, and submitted photos in the message body. The private AI assessment described below is included as a separate PDF attachment. When you provide an email address, it is used as the Reply-To address so the store can respond directly through its email app. Staff may also use your other provided contact channels.

We send the submitted photos and notes to OpenAI to help group items, describe visible condition, identify missing information, suggest broad price ranges when there is enough evidence, and prepare a private assessment and reply draft. Price ranges are estimates, not appraisals or offers. We do not include the form's name, phone, or email fields in the AI request. Avoid including personal documents or unnecessary personal information in photos or notes. AI assessments may be inaccurate; staff make final acceptance decisions and choose what to send. The AI does not email consignors automatically. Suggested replies and price guidance are kept in the private AI Assessment PDF, separate from the message body that may be quoted in a reply. Our requests disable stored API responses; OpenAI may retain limited data for security and abuse monitoring under its service policies.

Website And Hosting Data

This website is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare. When the website is loaded, Cloudflare may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, referring page, timestamps, traffic-routing information, and error or security logs.

This information is used to deliver the website, maintain its reliability, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and protect the website and its visitors. The website code does not include dedicated analytics services or advertising pixels.

Third-Party Services

The home page includes a Google Maps embed and an Instagram highlights widget provided through LightWidget. These embeds load when you approach their section of the page. When they load, Google or LightWidget may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device details, referrer, and interaction data.

The website also links to services and websites operated by Facebook, Instagram, Google, the Urbandale Chamber of Commerce, and SimpleConsign. Those services receive information when you choose to follow a link to them.

Each third party handles information under its own privacy policy and terms. You can learn more from the privacy policies for Cloudflare, Google, LightWidget, Meta, OpenAI, and SimpleConsign.

Changing Places does not control the privacy practices of these third parties.

SimpleConsign Consignor Portal

This website links to the SimpleConsign consignor portal for consignor-related access. Information entered into or handled through the portal is processed through that separate service. Changing Places may receive or access information needed to administer the consignment relationship, while SimpleConsign handles portal information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Cookies And Local Storage

Changing Places' website code does not set its own cookies or use advertising pixels.

The Couch Dash game uses your browser's local storage to remember your high score, sound preference, and selected radio station on your device. This information stays in your browser and is not used by Changing Places to identify or track you. You can remove it by clearing this website's stored data in your browser.

Google Maps and LightWidget may set cookies or use similar technologies when their embedded content loads. You can limit this activity through your browser settings, privacy tools, or content-blocking features.

How Information Is Shared

Changing Places does not sell personal information collected through this website and does not use it for cross-site behavioral advertising.

Information may be handled by service providers when needed to operate and secure the website, provide a service you request, maintain the consignment relationship, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or complete a business transfer such as a sale or reorganization. The embedded and linked services described above process information under their own policies.

Retention

Web photo submissions are stored in Cloudflare services. Uploaded photos, contact information, notes, and private assessments are automatically deleted after 30 days. Incomplete uploads are removed after one day. Staff may delete submission data sooner. Notification emails and any separate correspondence are retained in the business mailbox, including attached photos and AI Assessment PDFs, according to the business's communication needs.

Separate communications and business records outside the web-submission system are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide services, manage the business relationship, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the business. Technical logs are retained according to the settings and practices of the hosting and security providers. Couch Dash preferences remain on your device until you clear them.

Security

Changing Places uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information it handles. However, no website, email, text message, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through ordinary email or text.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Changing Places does not knowingly collect personal information directly through this website from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Changing Places, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Your Choices

You can limit some third-party data collection by using browser privacy tools, blocking or disabling third-party cookies, or adjusting your device and browser settings.

You can also choose to contact Changing Places directly by phone, text, or email instead of interacting with embedded map or social content.

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information Changing Places maintains about you. These may include requesting access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, or objecting to certain uses. To make a request, email us with the subject line "Privacy Request." We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates are made, the "Last updated" date will be revised.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to ChangingPlacesDSM@gmail.com, or you can call 515-727-2117. Our mailing address is Changing Places Consignment Shop, 3250 100th St, Urbandale, IA 50322.
