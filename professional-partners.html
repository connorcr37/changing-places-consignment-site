Consign After Projects

Give Clients an Easy First Step

Have the client or project contact text Steph before moving anything. She'll guide the review and arrange a time for pieces we can accept.

Introduce the option

Present us as a curated showroom option for quality furniture and home decor. Appraisal, donation, whole-home liquidation, and specialty items can stay with the providers suited to those needs.

Start with a text

For a few items, send full views, any condition concerns, and the client's timeline to 620-255-8901. For a houseful or large project, describe the scope first—a huge photo packet may not be necessary.

Confirm the decision-maker

Keep the owner, client, executor, or other authorized party clear. They should understand the terms and know whose account will receive any payout.

Coordinate approved items

Steph will arrange the intake time. We don't provide in-house pickup, but we're happy to recommend a trusted full-service mover.

A Better Photo Packet

What Helps for Projects and Multiple Items

Share the basics below when practical. If the volume is large, text the scope first and let Steph tell you the easiest way to proceed.

  • One full, well-lit view of every item
  • Additional angles for large or unusually shaped pieces
  • Close-ups of damage, wear, repairs, or stains
  • Maker, retailer, or material information when known
  • Dimensions, quantities, and set components
  • Client name, decision-maker, project type, and general deadline

Set the condition standard before photos

Items should be clean, in good condition, and ready for our showroom—free of dirt, dust, stains, odors, and pet hair. Showing flaws helps Steph give the client a faster, clearer answer.

We currently consider furniture, rugs, mirrors, wall art, and home decor. We're not accepting armoires because of low demand.

What Clients Should Know

Clear Expectations Build Client Trust

A familiar process makes referrals easier and gives clients a clear idea of what happens next.

Every piece gets a fit review

Steph reviews condition, style, category, demand, and showroom space so clients get useful guidance before arranging transportation.

The showroom gives pieces time

We display approved items for up to four months, giving the right shopper time to discover them.

The payout is defined

Consignors receive 45% of the final sale price. Items over $60 follow a 15% markdown every 30 days, and payouts are issued the month after sale.

Consistent standards for every referral

Repeat relationships benefit from a familiar process and clear communication. Steph handles the early coordination; our showroom team handles pricing, presentation, and shoppers after intake.
