Source Distinctive Pieces From a Showroom That Changes Daily

Designers, realtors, home stagers, builders, property managers, senior move managers, and assisted-living coordinators can browse one-of-a-kind and practical furnishings without relying on a fixed catalog.

Visit with the project details in hand

  • Bring room, wall, doorway, hallway, stair, and elevator measurements.
  • Know the dimensions of the vehicle or mover that will carry the purchase.
  • Keep client priorities, palette notes, and material preferences easy to reference.
  • Follow Facebook and Instagram for recent highlights, then visit to see what's still available.

Daily turnover creates fresh sourcing opportunities on every visit. When a piece fits the project, plan to decide during the visit: purchases are generally first-come, first-served, and the store usually cannot hold an item for later purchase or a future markdown.

Plan transport before buying

Changing Places doesn't offer delivery. Staff can typically help load at the store, and a trusted full-service mover may be recommended for pieces that need professional handling.

Purchased items can usually remain at the store for 7–10 days, but confirm the pickup window with the front desk before purchase — especially when coordinating a client, installer, or mover.

Consign After Projects

Make Every Client Referral Clear From the Start

The best client experience begins with accurate expectations: photo review is required, approval is selective, and transport is separate.

Introduce the option

Present Changing Places as a curated showroom channel for appropriate furniture and home decor. Keep appraisal, donation, whole-home liquidation, and other specialist work with the providers best suited to those needs.

Organize the photos

Send full views, details, dimensions, maker information, condition concerns, item quantity, and the client's timeline to 620-255-8901.

Confirm the decision-maker

Keep the owner, client, executor, or other authorized party clear. They should understand the terms and know whose account will receive any payout.

Coordinate approved items

Arrange intake and transportation only after approval. Changing Places has no in-house pickup, but a trusted full-service mover may be recommended.

A Better Photo Packet

What to Include for Projects and Multiple Items

A structured submission makes it easier to review a group without losing track of condition, scale, or ownership.

  • One full, well-lit view of every item
  • Additional angles for large or unusually shaped pieces
  • Close-ups of damage, wear, repairs, or stains
  • Maker, retailer, or material information when known
  • Dimensions, quantities, and set components
  • Client name, decision-maker, project type, and general deadline

Set the condition standard before photos

Items should be clean, in good condition, and ready for the showroom — free of dirt, dust, stains, odors, and pet hair. Showing flaws honestly is more useful than hiding them; the team can make a faster, more informed fit decision.

Changing Places currently considers furniture, rugs, mirrors, wall art, and home decor. Armoires are not currently accepted because of low demand.

What Clients Should Know

Clear Expectations Build Client Trust

A consistent process helps professionals make confident referrals while keeping each item's approval, pricing, and timing clear from the start.

Every piece gets a fit review

Photo review considers condition, style, category, current demand, and showroom space so clients receive useful guidance before arranging transportation.

The showroom gives pieces time

Approved items are displayed for up to four months, creating time for professional presentation and the right shopper to discover them. Exact sale timing remains item-specific.

The payout is defined

Consignors receive 45% of the final sale price. Items over $60 follow a 15% markdown every 30 days, and payouts are issued the month after sale.

Consistent standards for every referral

Repeat relationships benefit from a familiar process and clear communication. Each item is reviewed under the same current terms, with approval, pricing, intake, and sale timing determined by the individual piece.
