Consign After Projects

Give Clients an Easy First Step

Have the client or project lead text us before moving anything. We'll review the pieces and arrange a time for anything we can accept.

Introduce the option

Present us as an easy showroom option for quality furniture and home decor. Appraisers, donation groups, estate-sale teams, and specialists can handle the rest.

Start with a text

For a few items, send full views, any condition concerns, and the client's timeline to 620-255-8901. For a houseful or large project, describe the scope first — a huge photo packet may not be necessary.

Confirm the decision-maker

Keep the owner, client, executor, or other authorized party clear. They should understand the terms and know whose account will receive any payout.

Plan the drop-off

We'll arrange the intake time. We don't provide in-house pickup, but we're happy to recommend a trusted full-service mover.

A Better Photo Packet

What Helps for Projects and Multiple Items

Share the basics below when you can. If you've got a lot of pieces, text us a quick overview first and we'll tell you the easiest way to get started.

  • One full, well-lit view of every item
  • Additional angles for large or unusually shaped pieces
  • Close-ups of damage, wear, repairs, or stains
  • Maker, retailer, or material information when known
  • Dimensions, quantities, and set components
  • Client name, decision-maker, project type, and general deadline

Set the condition standard before photos

Items should be clean, in good condition, and ready for our showroom — free of dirt, dust, stains, odors, and pet hair. Showing flaws helps us give the client a faster, clearer answer.

We currently consider furniture, rugs, mirrors, wall art, and home decor. We're not accepting armoires because of low demand.

What Clients Should Know

Clear Expectations Build Client Trust

A familiar process makes referrals easier and gives clients a clear idea of what happens next.

We review every piece first

Our team looks at condition, style, category, demand, and showroom space so clients get a useful answer before spending time moving anything.

The showroom gives pieces time

We display approved items for up to four months, giving the right shopper time to discover them.

The payout is defined

Consignors receive 45% of the final sale price. Items priced at $60 and over are marked down by 15% of the original price every 30 days, and payouts are ready on the 5th of the month after a sale.

Consistent standards for every referral

Repeat projects are easier with a familiar process. Our team handles the review, pricing, presentation, shopper questions, and sale from start to finish.
