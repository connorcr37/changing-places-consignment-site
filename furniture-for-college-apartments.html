Decide Before Move-In Day

Separate Shared Needs From Personal Rooms

A quick roommate plan keeps the budget clear and makes it easier to choose together.

Students and roommates

List who's bringing what and who will own shared purchases. Have one person save the measurements and another line up the vehicle.

Parents helping furnish

Agree on a total budget and the pieces that matter most for comfort, study, storage, and shared meals. If you can, shop together so you can confirm fit, budget, and pickup in one trip.

Graduate and professional students

Look beyond one lease. A well-constructed desk, table, chair, lamp, or storage piece may suit a future apartment or first post-school home if its size stays flexible.

Apartment-Friendly Thinking

Find Pieces That Work Now and Later

Our selection changes daily, so look for pieces that fit this apartment and can move easily to the next one.

  • Proportions that leave clear walkways in smaller rooms
  • Pieces that can serve more than one purpose
  • Construction and condition that you've inspected in person
  • Shapes that can move through apartment doors, halls, and stairs
  • Sizes likely to work in more than one future layout
  • A look that reflects the students who live there

See the quality for yourself

Secondhand shopping lets you compare materials, weight, finish, and wear in person. Open the drawers, test the chair, and look it over — we want you to feel good about what you take home.

A Smarter Furnishing Budget

Spend Where It Matters Most

Start with what everyone will use every day. The finishing touches can come later.

Set the total

Include furniture, getting it home, cleaning supplies, household basics, and any building move-in costs in one realistic number.

Buy the everyday basics first

Start with seating, a place to eat or study, lighting, and useful storage based on the apartment and what everyone already owns.

Leave room for finds

Save a little of the budget for a rug, mirror, art piece, or table that adds personality when the right one appears.

Write down who owns what

A quick note now saves time and arguments when the lease ends.

Move-In Logistics

Measure the Apartment and the Vehicle

Bring building measurements

Measure the room, doorway, hall, stairwell, elevator opening and interior, plus any tight turns. Confirm building rules and move-in times before shopping.

Confirm the pickup plan

Measure the vehicle's cargo opening and usable interior. We can usually help load your vehicle.

Ask about short storage

We can usually keep a purchase for 7–10 days. Confirm with us before checkout, or ask for a trusted mover recommendation.

Come ready to decide. Most pieces are first come, first served. Measurements and roommate decisions make it easy to say yes when the right one appears.
