Furniture Consignment Guide

Sell Your Furniture Without Turning It Into a Second Job

Changing Places offers a managed way to sell quality furniture and home decor: our team handles in-store pricing, presentation, shopper questions, and the sales conversation while you stay out of the daily back-and-forth.

A Managed Service

What Changing Places Handles for You

Consignment is not simply a place to post a listing. It moves the customer-facing work into a staffed showroom built for furniture and home decor.

Pricing and presentation

Our team evaluates each approved piece in person and prices it using condition, brand, style, current demand, and resale experience. We then present it on the sales floor.

Shopper exposure

Your item can be discovered by people who already visit Changing Places to shop for furnishings, including destination shoppers who travel from communities several hours away, rather than depending only on a single online listing.

Buyer communication

Staff answers shopper questions and handles the transaction. You do not have to respond to repeated messages, negotiate with each buyer, or invite strangers into your home.

The Process

From Photos to Payout

Approval comes before delivery, and final pricing happens only after the team sees an item in person.

Send photos

Text clear photos of the furniture or home decor to 620-255-8901. Include any condition concerns that may not be obvious in a photo.

Confirm showroom fit

The team reviews fit, style, condition, demand, and available showroom space, then identifies the pieces approved for a scheduled drop-off.

Arrange transport

Approved items are scheduled for drop-off during store hours. Changing Places does not provide in-house pickup, but a trusted full-service mover may be recommended.

Follow the sale

Track items in the consignor portal. If an item sells, your 45% share is paid on the 5th of the following month under the store's current payout terms.

What May Be a Fit

Accepted Items Start With Condition and Demand

Changing Places considers clean, gently used furniture and home decor that is ready for the sales floor. Current categories include furniture, rugs, mirrors, wall art, and home decor.

  • Clean and free of dirt, dust, stains, odors, and pet hair
  • In good condition, with useful details visible in the photos
  • A style, category, size, and price point that fit current shopper demand
  • Ready to move to the showroom if approved

Approval is always item by item

Space and demand change. Photo review does not guarantee final acceptance, and the team confirms pricing only after seeing an item in person. Changing Places is not currently accepting armoires because of low demand.

If a piece is not a fit, that does not necessarily mean it lacks value; a private sale, estate sale, auction, donation, or another specialty venue may be better for that item.

Terms and Timing

Know What Happens After an Item Arrives

Consignment trades some control and a share of the sale price for professional handling and fewer logistical demands on the seller.

Pricing and markdowns

Changing Places prices items using brand, condition, style, trends, and customer demand. Items priced over $60 are marked down by 15% every 30 days under the current schedule.

Time on the floor

Items are displayed for up to four months. Some sell sooner and some do not sell at all. Consignment is not a guaranteed fit for a hard move-out deadline.

Payouts and unsold items

Consignors receive 45% of the final sale price. Payouts are issued on the 5th of the month after a sale: under $200 in cash and $200 or more by check, picked up in store.

At the end of the term

If an item remains unsold after four months, you may renew it for another term or arrange pickup. Items that are not renewed or picked up are released to the store and may be further discounted, donated, or otherwise handled at its discretion; released items are no longer eligible for pickup or payout.

Is Consignment Right for You?

A Strong Fit When Your Time and Privacy Matter

Consignment can be especially useful when you have saleable pieces but do not want to run the entire selling process yourself.

  • Busy households
  • Movers and relocations
  • Retirees and empty nesters
  • Families handling estates
  • People combining households
  • Design and real estate clients
  • Anyone avoiding in-home buyer visits

Consignment may suit you if…

  • You would rather share the final sale price than manage listings and buyers.
  • You can transport approved pieces or hire a mover.
  • Your timeline allows an item time to find the right shopper.

Another option may suit you if…

  • You need everything removed by a firm date.
  • You want complete control over price, negotiation, and buyer selection.
  • You have a full household or many items outside the store's categories.
