Less Work for You

What We Handle for You

Your pieces get a place on our showroom floor — and you get to skip listings, buyer messages, negotiations, and appointments at home.

Pricing and presentation

We price each approved piece in person using its condition, brand, style, and current demand, then present it on our sales floor.

Shopper exposure

Your item can be seen by people who already visit us to shop for furnishings, including shoppers who travel from several hours away.

Buyer communication

We answer questions and handle the transaction. You don't have to manage repeated messages, negotiate with buyers, or invite strangers into your home.

The Process

From Photos to Payout

Getting started takes one text. We'll confirm what may work before you make plans to move anything.

Text us a few photos

Send clear photos of your furniture or home decor to 620-255-8901. A full view and a quick photo of any wear are a great start.

Hear what may be a fit

We'll let you know what we're able to accept and arrange a drop-off time for approved pieces.

Bring in approved pieces

Bring them at the arranged time. Need moving help? We're happy to recommend a trusted full-service mover.

Follow the sale

Track your items in the consignor portal. If something sells, your 45% share is ready on the 5th of the following month under our current payout terms.

What May Be a Fit

What We're Looking For

We consider clean, gently used furniture and home decor that is ready for the sales floor, including rugs, mirrors, wall art, and decorative pieces.

  • Clean and free of dirt, dust, stains, odors, and pet hair
  • In good condition, with useful details visible in the photos
  • A style, size, and category that fit what shoppers are looking for now
  • Ready to move to the showroom if approved

Have a whole house or a large group?

Text us with a rough idea of what you have. You may not need to photograph every piece — we may be able to stop by and quickly tell you what could work in the showroom.

For smaller groups, our team reviews each piece based on current space and shopper demand. We're not accepting armoires right now.

Terms and Timing

Know What Happens After an Item Arrives

Here are the practical details, so you know what to expect after your pieces arrive.

Pricing and markdowns

We price items using brand, condition, style, and customer demand. Items priced at $60 and over are marked down by 15% of the original price every 30 days.

Time on the floor

Items are displayed for up to four months, though most sell sooner. If you have a firm move-out date, tell us when you text.

Payouts and unsold items

You receive 45% of the final sale price. Payouts are ready on the 5th of the month after a sale: under $200 in cash and $200 or more by check, picked up in store.

At the end of the term

If an item is still here after four months, you can renew it or arrange pickup. Items that aren't renewed or picked up are released to us and are no longer eligible for pickup or payout.

Is Consignment Right for You?

A Good Fit When Your Time and Privacy Matter

Consignment can be especially helpful when you have quality pieces but don't want to run the sale yourself.

  • Busy households
  • Movers and relocations
  • Retirees and empty nesters
  • Families handling estates
  • People combining households
  • Design and real estate clients
  • Anyone avoiding in-home buyer visits

Consignment may suit you if…

  • You'd rather share the final sale price than manage listings and buyers.
  • You can transport approved pieces or hire a mover.
  • Your timeline allows an item time to find the right shopper.

Another option may suit you if…

  • You need everything removed by a firm date.
  • You want complete control over price, negotiation, and buyer selection.
  • You have many items outside our furniture and home decor categories.
